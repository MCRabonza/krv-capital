Every Asian Merchant House Had One.

Held the family's money and ran its business; at the great houses, counsel too. Asia never called him an accountant.

Japan
Bantō
番頭

Ran the merchant house and held its money. Mitsui's bantō founded Mitsui Bank. Sumitomo's saved its copper mine, by his own rule: “to defy an order for the master's good is true loyalty.”

China
Zhanggui
掌柜

Ran the firm and held its money while the owner put up the capital. The Shanxi bank managers held shares and voted on lending.

India
Munim
मुनीम

Trusted accountant-manager of the trading house, keeper of the bahi-khata. Its agents ran the branches, often for one family across generations.

Thailand
Long-ju
หลงจู้

Teochew for the manager of the mill, the shop or the trading house, as distinct from its owner, the thao-kae.
