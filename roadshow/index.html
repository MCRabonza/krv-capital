06The Ask

THB 15M Working Capital.

  1. THB 1.5M minimum ticket.
  2. 15% of KRV & Co.’s gross service revenue, shared pro rata and paid monthly from March 2027.
  3. Paid until 1.5x, then closed: THB 2.25M back on each THB 1.5M. No interest, no equity.
